Protein & Food Calculator based on Brenda's Guidelines:

🥩 Meat, Poultry & Fish:

  • 100g Chicken / Turkey Breast: ~30g protein
  • 100g Salmon / White Fish Fillet: ~22–25g protein
  • 100g Lean Beef: ~26–28g protein
  • Canned Tuna (~100g drained): ~25g protein
  • 100g Breaded Schnitzel / Meatballs (processed): ~12–18g protein

🧀 Dairy & Cheeses:

  • Cottage Cheese 5% (Half a tub - 125g): ~14g protein
  • 100g Protein-enriched Yogurt: ~20g protein

  • 1 cup of bio yogurt (150 g): 6g protein
  • 50g Gouda cheese: 12g protein
  • 50g Roquefort cheese: 11g protein
  • 2 slices of yellow cheese (28% fat): 10g protein

🍎 Fresh Fruits (Pieces):

  • Banana: 1/2 large banana (or 1 very small)

  • Grapes: 12 medium grapes
